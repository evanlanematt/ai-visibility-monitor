How the AI Visibility Monitor gets a business recommended

Every week it asks AI assistants the questions real customers ask, finds out who gets recommended instead and why, works through the fixes, and proves what moved.

What happens once a client says yes

1. The owner gives accessA 30-minute intake call: manager access to their Google profile, a website login, and their real services, prices and story. Entered once in Fix plan › Business facts.
2. The tool does the workIt builds the fix plan, writes every profile, page, schema block, review request and outreach pitch from the owner's real facts, and tracks each task.
3. The owner does their partOnly what has to come from the business: verification codes, asking customers for reviews, and local relationships. Each is a task with clear instructions.

The six fix areas

Each area maps to a part of the tool. Tasks marked done are added to the trend chart automatically, so you can see which fix moved the numbers.

1

Listings

AI assistants with search pull local businesses from listing data first: Google, Bing, Apple and booking sites like Fresha.

The tool does

  • Writes the Google profile: description, categories, services, Q&A, first posts
  • Writes matching Bing, Apple and booking-site copy (with French versions in Quebec)
  • Flags which booking sites the AI cites in your area

The owner does

  • Claims and verifies each listing (codes go to the business)
  • Adds the agency as a manager

In the tool: Assets › Google profile & listings, Fix plan › Listings

2

Website

The website is where AI checks what the business actually does, so every page should be clear, specific and quotable.

The tool does

  • Drafts a page per service with prices, plus an FAQ page and titles
  • Generates LocalBusiness schema from the owner's facts
  • Checklist for crawler access and speed

The owner does

  • Confirms prices and details
  • Gives a website login, or approves a simple new site

In the tool: Assets › Website pages, Assets › Schema markup

3

Reviews

Reviews decide who gets named for "best" and "top-rated" questions: how many, how recent, and what they mention.

The tool does

  • Writes the text and email asks, follow-up and staff script
  • Writes reply templates for good and bad reviews

The owner does

  • Sends the ask after each visit, to real customers only
  • No incentives, no fake reviews

In the tool: Assets › Review requests

4

Content

Every question where the business was missing becomes a page that answers it directly, with details only this business has.

The tool does

  • Turns missed questions into pages: answer first, real prices and details
  • Keeps them few and specific, never mass-produced

The owner does

  • Shares real details and photos in the intake
  • Approves before anything is published

In the tool: Fix plan › Content, Results › Question by question

5

Links

Other trusted sites mentioning the business is what moves it from "known" to "recommended". These links have to be earned, never bought.

The tool does

  • Finds targets: sites the AI cites, local associations, roundups, partners
  • Writes a personal pitch for each, tracks status and follow-up dates
  • Weekly research adds new real targets

The owner does

  • Joins the local association, sponsors, builds partner relationships
  • Approves pitches sent in their name

In the tool: Links

6

Tracking

The same questions every week, so the trend is real. Every completed fix is marked on the chart.

The tool does

  • Re-asks every question each Monday with live web search
  • Shows mention rate, position, share of voice and cited sites
  • Marks each finished task on the trend chart

The owner does

  • Nothing. It runs on its own.

In the tool: Results

Rules the tool follows

  • Everything is written from the owner's real facts. Missing facts are marked [ADD: …], never invented.
  • No fake reviews, no incentives for reviews, no bought links, no mass-produced AI pages.
  • Anything published or sent in the owner's name is approved by the owner first.
  • No one can guarantee what an AI recommends. The tool promises the work and proves the results.
